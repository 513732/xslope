How to interpret this budget

Boundary inflow and outflow are positive magnitudes at selected external boundary nodes. For a pumped pit, read boundary outflow. Internal exchange is signed positive into the selected nodal set. Storage gain is positive. The residual equals boundary inflow − boundary outflow + internal exchange − storage gain. An interior set can exchange water and change storage even when external flows are zero.

Selection is a union of node IDs. Selecting an edge includes its end nodes and any midside node. The budget includes each selected node’s complete assembled reaction. At a shared corner, an isolated edge therefore has no unique edge-specific share. Internal exchange is the net conductive contribution to those finite-element rows, not a reconstructed normal flux across a user-drawn line.

Flow rates are step averages assigned to the end of each accepted interval. Volumes integrate every accepted step. Head and pressure are sampled at saved frames; choose individual node histories or their unweighted mean and range. Steady head and pressure plots show node values against distance or elevation. A steady solution has no cumulative volume. All flows and volumes refer to one unit of out-of-plane width.
